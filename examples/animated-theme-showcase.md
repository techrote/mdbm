# Animated Theme Showcase

This document is intentionally plain: the movement belongs to the theme, not the Markdown.
Open it while switching among the animated reference examples with **Theme → Choose theme**.

## 04 · Maintenance Ecosystem

A lunar-service maintenance bay with moving cable crawlers, pulse lamps, drifting relay moths,
falling coolant and a diagnostic underlay. Saving successfully produces a short divider arc.
Press **F8** to fire the user-cue arc burst directly.

## 05 · Relay Moth Swarm

A quieter theme built around the relay-moth motion language: wing-frame cycling, slow orbit drift,
ribbon traces, breathing density and sparse signal points. Focus changes briefly excite the swarm.

## 06 · Diagnostic Aurora

Layered service-weather bands move in opposing directions while prismatic coolant falls through the
outer artspace. Save success and failure cues produce finite arc-storm bursts; **F8** triggers the
manual storm cue.

## Motion policy

**Theme → Cycle motion intensity** switches among off, reduced, normal and high. Reduced and off
freeze or remove animated decoration without changing workspace behavior. Host-owned cursor,
selection, focus, menus, alerts and confirmations remain protected at every motion level.
